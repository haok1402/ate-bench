### Variable-k selection mask

PASS if the active-expert count varies per token, with the selection coming from a threshold comparison (boolean mask, `where`, or sigmoid-margin) rather than from `topk` over softmax. Credit attempts that still call `topk` downstream as a shape-static packing step (the published recipe), as long as the selection mask itself originates from threshold comparison.
FAIL if the only selection mechanism is `topk` over a softmax-derived score with no threshold-based gating.
