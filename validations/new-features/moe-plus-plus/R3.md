### Load-balancing aux loss handles zero experts

PASS if the load-balancing auxiliary loss has explicit handling for zero experts: excluded from the balance term, weighted differently, or balanced under a separate penalty. Look for `aux_loss`, `load_balance`, or `balance_loss` references where the zero experts are treated specially.
FAIL if the standard load-balancing loss is applied unchanged across the entire (regular + zero) expert pool with no special handling for the zero variants.
