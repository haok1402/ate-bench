### Q/K projection split into two halves

PASS if Q and K are partitioned into two halves before the differential attention call, by either:
- Doubling the Q/K projection output dimension and splitting via `chunk(2)` / `split` / `view`, OR
- Splitting the existing per-head Q/K dim in half before two parallel attention calls.
FAIL if there is no Q/K splitting at all.
